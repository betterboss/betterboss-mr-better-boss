How It Works

  1. Navigate to any job, proposal, or contract in JobTread
  2. Click the DocFill button (bottom-right corner)
  3. Choose your template, fill in client details
  4. Click Copy Description, Copy Footer, or Copy All
  5. Paste directly into your JobTread document fields

Keyboard Shortcuts

Ctrl+Shift+BToggle DocFill panel
Ctrl+Shift+DCopy description
Ctrl+Shift+FCopy footer
